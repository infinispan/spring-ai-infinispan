= Supported filter operations

The {brandname} vector store supports the following Spring AI filter expression operations.

[%header,cols="1,2,2"]
|===
| Operation | Expression | Example

| Equals
| `==`
| `country == 'BG'`

| Not equals
| `!=`
| `status != 'active'`

| Greater than
| `>`
| `year > 2020`

| Greater or equal
| `>=`
| `score >= 4.5`

| Less than
| `<`
| `price < 100`

| Less or equal
| `\<=`
| `rating \<= 3`

| In
| `in`
| `country in ['BG', 'NL']`

| Not in
| `not in`
| `status not in ['deleted']`

| Is null
| `IS NULL`
| `year IS NULL`

| Is not null
| `IS NOT NULL`
| `year IS NOT NULL`

| And
| `&&`
| `country == 'BG' && year == 2020`

| Or
| `\|\|`
| `country == 'BG' \|\| country == 'NL'`

| Not
| `NOT(...)`
| `NOT(country == 'BG')`
|===
